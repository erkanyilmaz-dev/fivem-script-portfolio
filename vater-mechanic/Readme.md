# 🔧 vater-mechanic | Advanced Vehicle Tuning & Repair System

> **vater-mechanic** is an enterprise-grade, fully interactive vehicle tuning, repair, and mechanic management system built for FiveM servers. Designed with an immersive 3D diagnostic workbench, custom performance tuning, real-time part wear mechanics, and modern NUI menus, it provides the ultimate experience for both mechanic job roles and automated self-service garages.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **🏎️ Deep Performance & Visual Tuning:** Modify engine components, turbos, suspension systems, brakes, body kits, liveries, custom wheels, and neon lighting with real-time 3D camera previews.
* **🔧 Realistic Vehicle Wear & Diagnostics:** Dynamic vehicle part degradation (engine block, clutch, transmission, brakes, and suspension) requiring physical inspection and specialized tools to repair.
* **💻 Interactive NUI Diagnostics:** Advanced diagnostic tablet UI displaying live vehicle telematics, damage reports, performance specs, and repair cost estimations.
* **🛠️ Custom Stashes & Crafting:** Integrated workshop stash management and item-based crafting systems for mechanic tools, spare parts, and upgrade kits.
* ** Dyno Testing & Performance Stats:** Measure horse power, top speed, acceleration curves, and braking distance directly inside mechanic shops.
* **⚡ Ultra-Optimized:** Runs at `0.00ms` on idle with zero client-side performance drop during extensive vehicle modifications.
* **🌐 Job & Public Compatibility:** Configurable to operate as player-owned mechanic shops (LSC, Bennys, Hayes, etc.) or standalone public repair stations.

---

## 📦 Dependencies

Ensure the following resources are installed on your server prior to running this script:

* **Framework:** `qb-core` or `es_extended` (ESX)
* **Inventory:** `ox_inventory`, `qb-inventory`, or metadata-compatible inventories
* **Target System:** `qb-target` or `ox_target`
* **Database:** `oxmysql`
* **UI / Utility:** `ox_lib`
