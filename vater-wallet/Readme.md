# 👛 vater-wallet | Ultra-Realistic & Interactive Personal Wallet System

> **vater-wallet** is an ultra-realistic, highly detailed personal wallet and document management system built for FiveM servers. Designed with physical wallet inspection animations, modern glassmorphic NUI graphics, dynamic ID card rendering, real-time credit card transactions, and physical photo inserts, it brings unprecedented realism and depth to character interactions and roleplay scenarios.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **🪪 Dynamic 3D Physical ID Cards:** Generate realistic, visually detailed driver's licenses, police badges, weapon permits, and citizen IDs complete with character mugshots, signatures, and dynamic registration dates.
* **💳 Realistic Credit & Debit Cards:** Use physical credit cards linked to bank accounts, complete with PIN verification, custom bank branding, card numbers, and tap-to-pay (NFC) interaction mechanics at registers and stores.
* ** Physical Cash & Compartments:** Inspect cash inside distinct wallet slots, allowing players to hand over physical bills, show cash totals, or stash hidden items like business cards and small notes.
* **🖼️ Personal Photos & Collectibles:** Insert custom personal photo items (e.g., character family pictures, polaroids) directly into wallet slots to view anytime via the interactive NUI.
* **🔍 Proximity ID Showing & Handover:** Show or physically hand your ID cards, licenses, or wallet to nearby players with fluid 3D animations and full NUI inspection for the receiving player.
* **⚡ Ultra-Optimized Engine:** Operates at `0.00ms` on idle, utilizing fast NUI caching and asynchronous rendering for instant UI responses.
* **💼 Business Cards & Notes:** Store, hand out, and read interactive business cards and scribbled paper notes kept inside the wallet.

---

## 📦 Dependencies

Ensure the following resources are installed on your server prior to running this script:

* **Framework:** `qb-core`, `es_extended` (ESX), or Standalone
* **Inventory:** `ox_inventory`, `qb-inventory`, or metadata-compatible inventories
* **Banking (Optional):** `qb-banking`, `okokBanking`, or standard framework banks
* **UI / Utility:** `ox_lib`
